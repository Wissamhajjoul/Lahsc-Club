Where Heritage Meets the Field

LAHSC was founded to bring people together through hunting, shotgun sports, and shared outdoor traditions. The club connects members from different backgrounds and countries, creating a welcoming community where experienced shooters, hunters, and beginners can learn from one another.

Who We Are

A Club Built on Brotherhood & Sport

Our club introduces new members to the three main parts of our community: shotgun shooting from Skeet to Trap and, Sporting clays, and hunting. Many members begin with little or no experience, then grow through practice, guidance, and competition. From beginner and hunter class shooters to advanced competitors, including AA and Master class levels, LAHSC gives every member a place to improve, compete, and belong.

🎯

Sporting Excellence

From sporting clays to trap shooting and tournaments, we challenge and reward members at every level.

📚

Safety & Education

Firearm safety courses, hunter education, and responsible ownership for all ages and experience levels.

🤝

Community & Heritage

Celebrating Lebanese roots and American traditions — at the range, in the field, and at the table.
